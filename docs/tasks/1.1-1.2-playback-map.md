## A. Playback state exposure

It implements `PlaybackStateManager.Listener`; `PlaybackStateManager` is the central state controller, and `PlaybackStateHolder` is the player-facing source of truth. ## D. Starting playback from a collection

The UI-facing collection methods are:

- `PlaybackViewModel.play(album: Album)` and `shuffle(album: Album)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:331-349`)
- `PlaybackViewModel.play(artist: Artist)` and `shuffle(artist: Artist)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:351-369`)
- `PlaybackViewModel.play(genre: Genre)` and `shuffle(genre: Genre)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:371-389`)
- `PlaybackViewModel.play(playlist: Playlist)` and `shuffle(playlist: Playlist)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:391-409`)
- `PlaybackViewModel.play(songs: List<Song>)` and `shuffle(songs: List<Song>)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:411-429`)

Detail screens call the parent-object overloads directly, e.g. album, artist, genre, and playlist. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/detail/AlbumDetailFragment.kt:154`, `app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/detail/ArtistDetailFragment.kt:184`, `app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/detail/GenreDetailFragment.kt:148`, `app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/detail/PlaylistDetailFragment.kt:250`)

The command factory accepts parent objects through `PlaybackCommand.Factory.album(album: Album, shuffle: ShuffleMode)`, `artist(artist: Artist, shuffle: ShuffleMode)`, `genre(genre: Genre, shuffle: ShuffleMode)`, and `playlist(playlist: Playlist, shuffle: ShuffleMode)`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/state/PlaybackCommand.kt:51-74`)

The resulting `PlaybackCommand` contains:

- `song: Song?`
- `parent: MusicParent?`
- `queue: List<Song>`
- `shuffled: Boolean`

(`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/state/PlaybackCommand.kt:38-49`)

Thus, starting from an album, artist, genre, or playlist takes a parent object; the factory derives and sorts its song list. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/state/PlaybackCommand.kt:103-135`, `app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/state/PlaybackCommand.kt:158-188`)

For starting at a specific song, the call is `PlaybackViewModel.play(song: Song, with: PlaySong)`, where `PlaySong` selects `FromAll`, `FromAlbum`, `FromArtist`, `FromGenre`, `FromPlaylist`, or `ByItself`. (`app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaybackViewModel.kt:217-267`, `app/src/main/java/org/oxycblt/auxio/playback/PlaySong.kt:38-87`)
